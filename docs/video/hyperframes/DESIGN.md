# Codex Control Center Video Design

## Style Prompt

Simple technical product videos for a local-first developer dashboard. Use a dark
ink canvas, restrained app panels, crisp type, and cyan-blue focus accents from
the JQAI Systems logo. The result should feel calm, safe, and practical rather
than cinematic or flashy.

## Colors

- `#050810`: background ink
- `#0f1724`: muted panel
- `#1f2a3a`: panel border
- `#18c6d4`: JQAI cyan accent
- `#7aa7ff`: Codex blue accent
- `#f8fbff`: primary text
- `#aab7c8`: secondary text

## Typography

- Display and UI text: generic `sans-serif`
- Technical labels: generic `monospace`
- Use strong size contrast and tabular numbers for timings or stats.

## Motion

- Use smooth push/crossfade transitions between scenes.
- Animate content in with short slide, scale, and opacity entrances.
- Keep motion subtle enough for screen-capture content to stay legible.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use private screenshots, private local paths, raw prompts, or logs.
- Do not create full-screen gradient-only scenes.
- Do not use large logo watermarks or busy decorative backgrounds.
- Do not show GitHub publishing as automatic.
